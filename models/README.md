# Models

A collection of parametric 3D-printable models and accessories.

- [AC Bayonet Connectors](./ac-bayonet-connectors/README.md) - Quick-release bayonet connectors for joining AC hoses and attaching them to devices or window adapters.
- [AC Drain Pan](./ac-drain-pan/README.md) - A parametric condensate drain pan for portable air conditioners.
- [AC Hose Connector Intake](./ac-hose-connector-intake/README.md) - Window and door panel connectors for AC intake and exhaust hoses, including an exhaust deflection cover.
- [AC Intake Cover Side Extension](./ac-intake-cover-side-extension/README.md) - A side extension box for adapting an AC intake cover to a wider device.
- [Cabinet Plugs](./cabinet-plugs/README.md) - A chamfered, compliant plug that covers unused holes in kitchen cabinets.
- [Clothes Pin](./clothes-pin/README.md) - A single-piece, spring-free parametric clothespin.
- [Connectagon](./connectagon/README.md) - A hollow hexagonal tile with rib and groove connectors that interlock in a honeycomb pattern.
- [Eye Drops](./eye-drops/README.md) - A simple holder for eye-drop bottles.
- [japandi](japandi/README.md) - Minimalist desk and home accessories, including a cylindrical pen holder with a filleted inner floor, a rounded tray, a pyramidal candle holder, and a fluted tea-light holder.
- [Lamp Base for a LED Puck](./lamp-led-puck/README.md) - A support-free parametric lamp base for an LED puck light.
- [Reveal Sliders](./reveal-sliders/README.md) - A print-in-place math flash card whose slider reveals the answer.
- [Seasonal Pumpkin Tealight Holder](./seasonal/README.md) - A fluted, pumpkin-shaped holder for an LED tealight.
- [xmas](./xmas/README.md) - A fluted Christmas tree with a spiral ledge and a plug-in star topper.
- [Sample OpenSCAD model](./sample.scad) - A small example model for experimenting with OpenSCAD.
